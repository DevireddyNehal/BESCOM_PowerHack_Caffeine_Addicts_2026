Welcome. Here's how this works.

A transformer is "overloaded" when it carries more power than it is rated for. This tool helps you decide whether moving some load to a neighbour is safe.

  1. 1
    Choose a situation. Normal evening, a +25% demand surge, or heavy rain.
  2. 2
    Read the label and symbol. ✓ OK, ! Near limit, and ✕ Overloaded. Color is only an extra cue.
  3. 3
    Test a fix. Press Suggest the best fix or drag the sliders to move load. Re-check each written status.
  4. 4
    Decide. Approve is only enabled when no transformer would be overloaded. Everything is logged.
Plain-English glossary
DTR
: distribution transformer, the grey box on a pole or pad that steps 11 kV down to household voltage.
kVA
: how much load a transformer carries or is rated for.
Tie switch
: a normally-open link between two transformers' low-voltage networks, used to share load.
90% watchline
: our early-warning level, set before the transformer is actually full.
Risk indicator
: a simulated 0 to 1 demo value. It is not a live model output.
